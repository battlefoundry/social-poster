Unleash the Undead Warlord on Your Table!

July 24, 2026

Meet the Undead Warlord, fresh off the digital sculpting bench and ready to haunt your table. Whether you need a terrifying final boss for your high-level D&D campaign or a dark commander to lead your skeleton hordes in your favorite tabletop wargame, this sculpt brings pure dread. Imagine your party stepping into a dusty tomb only to see glowing blue eyes ignite in the darkness as this guy draws his greatsword.

When you toss this STL into your slicer, tilt him back about thirty degrees to keep those delicate cape details and sword edges crisp. If you are printing in resin, throw in a few extra light supports around the shoulder pads and the tip of the blade to avoid any disappointing misprints. He prints like a dream on standard resin settings and scales down nicely if you want a smaller squad leader.

For painting, you do not need to overcomplicate things to get a stellar tabletop look. Prime him in black, give the armor a heavy drybrush of oily silver, and slather on a dark brown ink wash for that weathered, ancient look. Finish off the glowing eyes and runes with a dab of bright turquoise over white, and you have a miniature that looks straight out of an epic fantasy art book.
