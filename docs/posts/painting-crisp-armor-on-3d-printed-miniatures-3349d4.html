Painting Crisp Armor on 3D Printed Miniatures

August 04, 2026

Start with clean 3D printed miniatures. Wash your resin print thoroughly before curing. Coat the model in matte black primer. Spray a light neutral grey from a forty-five degree angle. Finish with a pure white spray directly from above. This zenithal prime maps your shadows before you open a single paint pot.

Basecoat the plate armor with a thin dark iron acrylic. Never apply thick paint straight from the bottle. Thin your paint with a drop of water on a flat palette. Shade the armor recesses using a dark wash. Let the wash dry completely. Lightly drybrush a bright silver along the sharpest edges of the metal.

Paint all leather straps and pouches using a deep reddish brown. Highlight the raised edges with a lighter tan to build quick contrast. Apply a thin brown wash over the leather sections. This step unifies the tones and creates realistic wear. Keep your brush control tight around the finished metal plates.

Finish the base to ready your tabletop RPG minis for the grid. Glue coarse sand around the feet with standard white glue. Paint the dry sand dark charcoal and drybrush it off-white. A clean base turns miniature painting projects into display-ready pieces for tabletop wargaming.
