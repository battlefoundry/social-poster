Dread Knight Greatsword Miniature

September 26, 2026

Varek rolls left across the shattered flagstones. The chained tombstone crushes the spot where he stood. Stone shrapnel tears through his cloak. The behemoth roars. The reaching hands inside its ribcage scrape empty air.

Heavy iron strikes stone at the broken archway. A second shadow falls across the chamber. A Dread Knight steps into the sickly green light. Spiked plate armor encases every inch of his massive frame. Melted wax binds strips of ancient parchment to his breastplate. He drags an eight-foot greatsword behind him. The notched steel throws sparks across the frozen floor.

The knight ignores the executioner and the wraith. He stops ten paces from the monster. Both gauntlets take the leather grip. He raises the slab of iron without straining. Runes carved into the fuller flare with cold violet flame.

The behemoth drops into a four-legged stance. Its chained hammer drags back for a killing sweep. The knight levels the burning blade at the beast's exposed chest. The stone between them begins to split.
