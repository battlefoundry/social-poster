The ash fell thick over the Iron Wastes. Varek did not slow his pace. His plate armor bore the dust of three fallen kingdoms. He pulled his greatsword from the frozen dirt.

Cold runes cut deep into the notched steel. Heavy pauldrons guard a neck scarred by ancient iron. A tattered war cloak drags through the red soil. He carries the crown of a dead king in his left gauntlet.

Shadows twist around the obsidian archway ahead. Something heavy wakes beneath the stone floor. The ground cracks beneath his boots. Varek shifts his grip and sets his stance.

A clawed hand breaks through the flagstones. Red eyes ignite in the black depth. Varek raises his blade. Tomorrow, the horde rises.
