Ironclad Grave Knight Undead Mini

July 24, 2026

The Ironclad Revenants once guarded the High Spires of Oakhaven. Their armor was forged from meteoric iron. They fought until the siege of the Third Dawn. None surrendered. Every soldier died inside his plate.

Death did not strip away their oath. The cold metal bound their souls to the steel. Now they walk the ash plains with heavy broadswords. They attack anything that breathes. They still execute forgotten standing orders.

A Revenant feels no pain. Fire scorches the flesh. The iron frame moves forward anyway. Strike through the visor slit to pierce the soul-flame inside. Otherwise, it grinds your vanguard into mud.
