Ash Knight Executioner Miniature

August 12, 2026

The Ash Knights do not bury their dead. They forge the ashes directly into plate armor. Every piece of iron carries the weight of a fallen brother. You do not earn this armor through rank. You inherit it through survival.

These warriors lead the vanguard into corrupted dead zones. Their heavy broadswords crush bone before the blade ever cuts flesh. They carry no shields. Fear is their only defensive line.

Enemy armies report hearing the armor hum before the line hits. That hum is the trapped heat of a hundred dead blacksmiths. When an Ash Knight falls, the squad collects the steel on the spot. The forge never cools.
