Ironclad Dread Chaplain

August 13, 2026

The Ashen Order built the Dread Chaplains to hold the eastern breaches. They forged their armor from cold iron and grave dust. No living heart beats inside the plate. A single trapped soul drives each suit forward.

They do not speak on the battlefield. They do not blow war horns or call for quarter. Instead, they chant iron litanies through brass vox-grilles. Enemies drop their weapons before the front lines even clash.

Ruin came to the Citadel six centuries ago. The living soldiers died or fled. The Chaplains remained. They still walk the shattered ramparts, waiting for an order that will never come.
