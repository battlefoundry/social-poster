How to Speed Paint Grimdark 3D Printed Miniatures

July 30, 2026

Start with a matte black primer. Drybrush the entire model heavily with a neutral gray. Finish with a light white drybrush strictly from above. This prep defines your light sources before color touches the resin.

Brush thin speed paint over the prepared surface. Transparent pigment over gray creates smooth shadow transitions. Highlights pop on top edges without manual blending. You build dark fantasy armor in minutes instead of hours.

Stipple bright silver onto high metallic edges with a dry brush. Dab dark brown paint along lower trim to simulate battlefield mud. Weathering hides minor print lines and adds immediate grit.

Seal the model with a flat matte varnish. Flat coats remove glossy resin glare under table light. Your tabletop RPG minis are ready for the gaming table.
