How to paint metallic armor on 3D printed miniatures

July 25, 2026

Layer lines ruin good paint jobs. Start your 3D printed miniatures with a heavy matte black primer. Spray a white zenithal coat from a high angle. This creates built-in shadows before paint touches the resin.

Apply a dark metallic base coat over the armor. Keep the paint thin so light shows through from the primer. Slather a dark brown wash over every recessed panel. Wipe the raised surfaces clean with a makeup sponge to leave deep shadows in the cracks.

Catch the hard edges with a bright silver drybrush. Focus only on top edges where light hits naturally. Add small rust spots near rivets using diluted orange paint. Your tabletop RPG minis will look battle-tested in twenty minutes.

Seal everything with an ultra-matte varnish. A dead-flat finish ties every layer together instantly. Your miniature painting process stays fast. Great free STL miniatures deserve a clean result on the table.
