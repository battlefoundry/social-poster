Grave Knight Heavy Infantry Mini

July 25, 2026

The Grave Knights do not bleed. They do not sleep. King Vane entombed his entire vanguard in sealed iron plates to hold the pass. Three centuries passed. The iron remains intact. The men inside turned to ash and spite.

They march without drums. No horns announce their arrival. Their heavy greatswords shatter steel shields in a single strike. Volleys of arrows ricochet off breastplates that carry no heart behind them.

Veterans call them the Rusted Wall. You cannot reason with armor full of cinders. You shatter their ranks with siege fire or you die in the mud. There is no middle ground.
