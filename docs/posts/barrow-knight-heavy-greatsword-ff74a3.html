Barrow Knight Heavy Greatsword

August 16, 2026

The Iron Vanguard do not carry shields. Their plate is two inches of cold-rolled iron. Nothing short of a ballista punches through it. They hold the gap when the rest of the line breaks.

Every suit outlives the soldier inside it. New recruits do not buy gear. They inherit it from the dead. Armorers rivet the cuirass shut the night before a siege. You survive the breach, or they chisel you out for the next recruit.

They fight with executioner blades taller than a man. They do not dodge or feint. They advance in silence and swing in heavy, rhythmic arcs that shatter shields on impact.

A general commits the Vanguard to end an engagement immediately. Once they march, there is no signal for retreat.
