A Classic Undead Captain for Your Next Dungeon Crawl

July 24, 2026

Imagine your party rounding a corner in a dusty tomb, expecting mindless skeletons, only to be confronted by an armored Skeletal Captain barking silent orders. This sculpt is perfect for a D&D boss encounter or as a unit champion leading a regiment in your favorite mass-battle fantasy wargame. The raised sword and battered shield give him an aura of ancient authority that immediately grabs attention on the grid.

When it comes to resin printing this guy, pay special attention to the thin bony wrists and sword blade. Angling the miniature back at roughly thirty degrees helps keep the heaviest support connections on the cape and legs, protecting the fine detail on his chest armor. If you are printing on FDM, slow down your outer walls to keep the ribcage details sharp and clear.

For painting, drybrushing is your absolute best friend with skeletal minis like this. Start with a deep reddish-brown base coat, heavy drybrush up to a bleached bone color, and give the armor plates a dab of orange ink for a grim, rusted finish. Slap a tiny drop of neon green paint into the eye sockets, and he is ready to terrorize your players.
