How to Paint Grimdark Armor on 3D Printed Miniatures

July 25, 2026

Clean your 3D printed miniatures before applying primer. Wash away all excess resin. Cure the resin completely under UV light. Apply a coat of matte black spray primer. Black hides hard-to-reach shadows on tabletop RPG minis.

Switch to a stiff, flat brush. Load a heavy grey paint onto the bristles. Wipe off most of the paint on a paper towel. Sweep the brush downward using fast, light strokes. Leave paint only on the raised edges.

Repeat the step with a light off-white paint. Focus only on the top edges where light hits naturally. Apply a dark brown oil wash over the entire miniature. Wipe the high surfaces clean with a makeup sponge.

This technique builds heavy contrast in minutes. It streamlines miniature painting for large army projects. Your tabletop wargaming models will look sharp on the field. Grab your free STL miniatures and test it tonight.
