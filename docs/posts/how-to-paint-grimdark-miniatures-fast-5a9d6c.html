How To Paint Grimdark Miniatures Fast

August 11, 2026

Prime your 3D printed miniatures in matte black. Spray a top-down zenithal highlight using a cool gray. This establishes instant shadows in every deep crease. Skip the smooth blends and let the sculpt texture work for you.

Heavy drybrush the armor with a dark gunmetal paint. Follow with a light silver stipple on the sharpest edges. Keep your brush almost completely dry. The raised details will catch the light immediately.

Apply a dark brown grime wash across the whole model. Let it set for five minutes. Take a moist cosmetic sponge and wipe the wash off the raised surfaces. Deep contrast remains trapped in the recesses.

Pick out lenses and glowing blades with a pure white base paint. Layer a bright neon glaze directly over the white. Your model is fully painted and ready for tabletop wargaming.
