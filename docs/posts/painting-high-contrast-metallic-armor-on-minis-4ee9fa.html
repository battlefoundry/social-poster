Painting High Contrast Metallic Armor on Minis

July 26, 2026

Basecoat your 3D printed miniatures with matte black spray primer. Black hides missed spots in tight recesses. Spray a bright white acrylic from directly above the model. This zenithal layer creates natural light maps before you open a paint pot.

Apply a dark metallic wash over the entire armor surface. Let the black primer do the heavy lifting in the deep shadows. Load a stiff drybrush with silver paint. Wipe ninety percent of the paint onto a paper towel. Flick the drybrush downward across every raised edge.

Mix black and brown shade washes together in equal parts. Coat the steel plates completely. The brown adds grease and wear to the mechanical joints. Finish with a flat matte varnish. Your tabletop RPG minis look battle-tested and ready for combat.
