Conquer the Crypt with the Skeletal Warlord

July 24, 2026

Hey fellow painters! Today we are diving into our latest fantasy release on BattleFoundry, the Skeletal Crypt Lord. Imagine your D&D party finally reaching the deepest vault of an ancient tomb, expecting a pile of dust, only to watch this towering undead champion rise from his throne with a massive glowing greatsword. He makes a fantastic end-of-dungeon boss or a menacing general for your tabletop undead armies.

On the wargaming table, this guy is built to command attention. Whether you are running a narrative campaign or a high-stakes skirmish game, his heavy plate armor and intimidating pose will strike fear into your opponents. You can practically feel the dark magic radiating off his blade as he marches toward the frontline.

For printing, try angling the model back slightly on your resin printer to keep those sharp sword edges looking clean without placing heavy supports along the blade. When painting, a dark metallic basecoat followed by a heavy wash gives the armor a great weathered look. Hit the bone areas with an off-white drybrush, then add a vibrant turquoise or bright green glaze to the eyes and sword runes to bring that magical glow to life.
